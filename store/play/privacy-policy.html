Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE 2026‑09‑30  ·  NO. 001

Finale has no account, no server of its own, and no analytics. It does talk to one outside service, The Movie Database (TMDB), to look up shows. Here's the whole picture.

01 — What we collect

Nothing.

Finale doesn't collect, sell, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics with us or anyone else. There's no sign-in, no user ID, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting service.

02 — What goes to TMDB

Only what's needed to look shows up.

To find shows and check their status, the app sends requests directly from your phone to TMDB:

  • the text you type into search,
  • the TMDB IDs of the shows you open or follow, to fetch their status and episode dates,
  • requests for poster images.

Like any website, TMDB receives your IP address with those requests. Nothing that identifies you (name, email, account) is sent, and we don't receive a copy. TMDB's handling of that traffic is covered by the TMDB privacy policy.

03 — Where your data lives

On your device.

The list of shows you follow is saved with your phone's operating system under the key finale:v1. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

04 — Backing up a lost phone

Handled by your phone, not by us.

Finale opts in to your device's own backup (iCloud Backup on iOS, "Back up to Google Drive" on Android). If you have that turned on, your watchlist is included; if it's off, it isn't. We never see that backup.

05 — Children

Not directed at children.

And nothing is collected from anyone, children included.

06 — Changes

You'll hear about it first.

If Finale changes in a way that touches this policy (for example, moving TMDB requests behind a server of our own), this policy will say so, with a new effective date, before that version reaches you.
